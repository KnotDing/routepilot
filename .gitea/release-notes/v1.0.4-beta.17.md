# RoutePilot v1.0.4-beta.17

## 中文

- 乐观决策缓存支持按 DIRECT、SURGE、REJECT 分别启用；乐观响应缓存支持按各 DNS 上游分别启用，覆盖全局、策略主上游、备用及已观察的原始目标。普通缓存保持原有行为。
- 缓存设置采用带总开关的分组框，勾选后显示各项乐观开关，取消后收起并保留分项设置；宽屏并排、手机上下排列。
- 决策缓存页与查询记录页新增分项条目、命中、待刷新和可靠性统计，包括已核对、不一致次数与不一致率。可靠性统计跨重启保留，手动清理缓存后重置；刷新失败不会记作一致。

## English

- Enable optimistic decision caching separately for DIRECT, SURGE and REJECT, and optimistic response caching by DNS upstream across global, policy, backup and observed original-destination resolvers. Ordinary caching keeps its existing behavior.
- Cache settings use grouped master switches. Enabling a master reveals per-item optimistic switches; disabling it collapses the group without resetting item preferences. Groups appear side by side on larger screens and stack on phones.
- Decision and query pages now show per-item entries, hits, pending refreshes and reliability, including verified requests, mismatches and mismatch rates. Reliability counters persist across restarts until manual cache clearing; failed refreshes are not counted as matching.
