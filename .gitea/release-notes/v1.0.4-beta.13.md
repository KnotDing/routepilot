# RoutePilot v1.0.4-beta.13

## 中文

- 客户端策略流向不再展示「未记录最终策略」的请求；各层、流带和明细同步排除，百分比基数也同步扣除，展示占比合计为 100%。
- 保留 DNS 请求流向、累计统计及独立策略排行的原有统计口径。

## English

- Client policy flows now exclude requests without a recorded final policy from all columns, ribbons, details and percentage denominators. Displayed shares sum to 100%.
- DNS request flows, cumulative counters and independent policy rankings retain their existing statistical scopes.
