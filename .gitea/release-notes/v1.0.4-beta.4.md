# RoutePilot v1.0.4-beta.4

## 监控来源展示调整

- 「来源请求占比」改为独立卡片，位于 DNS 请求流向和策略命中卡片下方，保留比例条及点击来源查看流向、策略命中的交互。
- 实时和历史视图的来源筛选、流向图及占比表隐藏「启用前未采集来源」项，不再展示无来源记录的旧请求。
- 旧累计统计继续保留，来源占比和流带比例的总量分母保持不变；页面注明来源视图只包含已采集来源的请求，避免将旧数据错归给客户端或重新放大占比。

## Monitoring source presentation

- Move **Source request shares** into its own card below the DNS flow and policy-hit cards, preserving proportion bars and source selection.
- Hide the pre-collection source entry in source filters, flow diagrams and share tables for both live and historical data.
- Preserve cumulative statistics and total denominators. Explain that source views show requests with recorded sources, without attributing old requests to clients or inflating their shares.
