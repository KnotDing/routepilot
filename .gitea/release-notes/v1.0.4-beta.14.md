# RoutePilot v1.0.4-beta.14

## 中文

- 客户端策略流向排除「未记录最终策略」的请求，各层、流带、精确明细和百分比分母同步调整。
- 明细数量较多时，未记录策略和容量溢出保留独立类型，不混入「其余策略」或「其余最终策略」，确保少量未记录请求也能准确排除。

## English

- Client policy flows exclude requests without a recorded final policy from all columns, ribbons, exact details and percentage denominators.
- Missing-policy and capacity-overflow observations retain distinct types when details are grouped, so small unrecorded flows are never folded into the remaining policies or included in their denominators.
