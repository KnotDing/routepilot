# RoutePilot v1.0.4-beta.12

## 中文

- 设置中新增「大屏监控」，可以逐项选择请求概况、流向、策略排行、来源占比及趋势等卡片的显示或隐藏。
- 新增「敏感数据保护」，用匿名标识展示客户端和策略，隐藏 IP、备注、后端名称及地址；来源筛选、悬停提示和明细同步保护，统计数量与流向保持准确。
- 大屏显示偏好在 App 与浏览器中保存，重新打开后保留。
- 修复客户端策略流向中第二段比例尺不一致的问题；三列节点数不同时，相同请求数的流带保持同宽，中间节点准确对齐。

## English

- Added Settings → Monitoring, with individual visibility controls for request summaries, flows, policy rankings, source shares and trends.
- Added sensitive data protection with anonymous client and policy labels. IPs, remarks, backend names and addresses are hidden in filters, tooltips and details while counts and flow proportions remain accurate.
- Monitoring display preferences persist in both the App and browser.
- Fixed inconsistent ribbon scaling and middle-node alignment when the three policy-flow columns contain different numbers of nodes.
